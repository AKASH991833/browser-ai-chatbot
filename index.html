A chatbot that lives in your browser

Intent matching, conversation memory, small talk, math, reminders-in-context and a teaching mode that lets you add new question-answer pairs - all running locally as plain JavaScript.

What I know

Built-in intents plus anything you teach me. Taught pairs persist in this browser.

Teach me

You can also teach inline: teach: when I say "hello team" you say "morning crew"

Honest limits

  • This is a pattern-matching chatbot, not a large language model. It answers what it was taught and handles its built-in intents; it does not reason about new topics.
  • Context memory is shallow - it remembers your name and the last topic within the session.
  • Taught pairs live in localStorage on this device only.
